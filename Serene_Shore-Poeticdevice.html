POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – waves, shore, openness.

Symbolism – shore as threshold between confinement and freedom.

Contrast – safety vs risk.

Mood – liberating, expansive.

Metaphor – shore as transition in life.

Personification – waves carrying possibility.

Alliteration – "blown with the breeze", "clan of clouds", 
"sails, strolling the seas", "revolt the rifles", "tune out there."

Tone – uplifting, tranquil, transforming.

Circumstantial perception – freedom altered by environment.

Theme – openness and transformation.

Rhyme scheme – free verse
