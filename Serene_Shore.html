🌊 SERENE SHORE

Out at the shore, released from the ties
Where I happen to be a passer-by,
I feel the heat blown with the breeze
Governing the clan of clouds in the sky.

I flow with the sails, strolling the seas,
The cradelling air upon my sleeves;
I blend with the riots of scarlet gradience
Dripping in the crystalline sapphire-
I revolt the rifles of ticking violence-
Inherit the tune out there.
