🍂 POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – golden rays, canopy crown.

Symbolism – autumn as grandeur and transition.

Metaphor – canopy as crown.

Tone – majestic, celebratory.

Mood – awe, reverence.

Contrast – fading light vs renewal.

Alliteration – "canopy crown", "bronze and brown", "yearnings of yearly", "breeze blows freckles and flakes"

Theme – beauty in impermanence.

Cycle motif – seasonal rhythm.

Personification – autumn light “highlights” grandeur.

Rhyme scheme - aabb (2nd stanza: abcb)
