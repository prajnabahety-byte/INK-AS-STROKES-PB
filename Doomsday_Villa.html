🪐 DOOMSDAY VILLA

In the vastness that rushes abound,
A lone villa stretches bound;
Where rivers deny welcome there,
Raided by poison everywhere.

The rusty duo of craters and dumps,
The friendly tune of forgotten drums,
The burnt tin roofs and grained bricks,
The skeletal forum and bundles of sticks.

The Martian era of ghostly views
With no freckle of life, a sign or clue;
All that remains is a dead land
Washed away with the breeze to sand.
