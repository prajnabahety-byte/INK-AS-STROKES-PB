EXPLANATION

The poem reflects a sense of freedom from worldly pressures and conflicts. The shore represents a space of reflection where the speaker stands between the structured human world and the limitless openness of nature. By describing themselves as a “passer-by”, the speaker suggests that human life is temporary and that people are only brief travellers through the world. The movement of the breeze, clouds, and sails symbolizes the natural flow of life, encouraging acceptance and calmness rather than control or struggle. The vivid blending of scarlet and blue in the sky highlights the beauty and harmony that exist in nature when one pauses to observe it. This peaceful imagery contrasts with the references to “rifles” and “ticking violence”, which represent tension, aggression, and the conflicts created by humanity. The speaker distances themselves from such violence and instead chooses to connect with the quiet rhythm of the natural world. Through this contrast, the poem suggests that peace and fulfillment can be found by stepping away from conflict and embracing the calm, flowing harmony of life and nature.
