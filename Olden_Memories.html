📸 OLDEN MEMORIES

One fine leisure night I decluttered a trunk
Brimming with decades old forgotten junk;
Admist the scratch a photo caught my eye,
Dusty and torn, which I taped with a sigh.

It happened to be of the golden old days
I enjoyed in the veranda of my house;
Where we laughed and played and quarrelled always
And raced in the rain and the scorching sun rays.

Neither in the parchments I wrote as a child,
Nor in the pictures embedded with dust mild,
But all memories reside in my heart
Which I wish to relive when I am broken apart.
