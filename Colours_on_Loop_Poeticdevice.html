POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – ruby, amber, crescent moon, mystical hues.

Symbolism – day and night cycles.

Metaphor – “infinite mile” as time.

Personification – celestial bodies “presenting” hues.

Repetition – first 2 and last 2 lines mirror each other, “file by file."

Alliteration – “infinite mile", "drowning visage of distant".

Assonance – "souffle of mould".

Tone – majestic, cosmic.

Mood – awe, reverence.

Cycle motif – endless repetition of day/night.

Theme – continuity and grandeur of celestial rhythm.

Rhyme scheme – abcb (3rd stanza: abac)
