POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – vivid description of the sun resting and colors of dusk.

Personification – the sun is given human qualities of “resting.”

Symbolism – sunset symbolizes closure and peace.

Assonance – "of the souffle that strolls."

Alliteration – "suspended sphere", "fading crackle of flakes", "sunset soul."

Metaphor – the sphere represents the sun.

Contrast – light fading into darkness.

Mood – serene, contemplative atmosphere.

Cycle motif – daily rhythm of sun rising and setting.

Visual metaphor – sky as canvas of colors.

Tone – celebratory of endings as beauty.

Rhyme scheme – free verse
