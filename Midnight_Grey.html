🌧️ MIDNIGHT GREY

Trickling drizzling drip-drip-drop,
Tears trodding down mist and fog,
Freckly pearls on intricate drapery
Of veils of pitch black midnight florency.

Smoky as clouds sway to-and-fro,
Guiding the milky essence hitherto
Rumbles of thunder brew out chaos
Followed by a glinting lightning lot.

Dim street lamps in the damp bleak lanes
Faint; lighting the streets with disdain.
The spotlight light that skiddles in this hue
Is the occasional march of the Moon into view.

As a silvery mystique descends upon
The frosty, misty zero kelvin, it spawns
Trickling drizzling drops into coldplay:
Of fifty shades of midnight grey.
