POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – burning sand, gale, barren soil.

Symbolism – desert as ruin of nature.

Metaphor – desert compared to ruins.

Personification – land “requests a drop of Nile.”

Alliteration – “flora is a misery, fauna raided”, "lifeless home of loneliness."

Assonance – "none holds authority."

Tone – stark, haunting.

Contrast – fertility vs barrenness.

Mood – desolate, solemn.

Theme – impermanence and fragility.

Parallelism – desert as natural ruin, ruins as human desert.

Rhyme scheme – 1.aabbcc 2.aabcc
