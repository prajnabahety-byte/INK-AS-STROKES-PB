🧳 POETIC DEVICES

Onomatopoeia – “jingling bells”, "tick-tick" mimic sound.

Imagery – souvenirs, suits, trinkets.

Symbolism – objects as memory of travel.

Metaphor – maps and books as new landscapes.

Tone – nostalgic, bittersweet.

Mood – longing, reflective.

Alliteration – "traveller traverses", "Sad reality just strikes", 
"souvenirs of his lifetime's stroll", "Miniatures and models", "traveller's trinkets"

Contrast – past freedom vs present limitation.

Theme – persistence of wandering spirit.

Personification – souvenirs “carry memory.”

Collection motif – trinkets as fragments of journeys.

Rhyme scheme – aabb (4th stanza: aabbcc)(5th: free verse)
