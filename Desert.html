🏜️ DESERT

A lifeless home of loneliness,
A chaotic silence of homeliness,
Where the sweepy gale toggles the land 
And strolls the burning sand,
Where the vast stretch of infinite miles
Requests a drop of Nile.

And the fury too hot as the burning ball
Which deserts the unfertile soil at all,
Where the flora is a misery, the fauna raided by treachery,
Where none holds authority,
In this unparallel land of sonority.
