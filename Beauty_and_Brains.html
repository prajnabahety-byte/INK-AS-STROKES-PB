🌻 BEAUTY AND BRAINS

A scarlet visage of distant amber
Adorned in sets of ruby and gold
And obedient fields of sunshine sunflowers
Slouching among the emerald growth.

Eerieness rendering within the woods
As nightfall drapes the ruby black,
Twinkling stars and squinting eyes
Flash yellow and white into the darkness.

As arises the visage out once again,
Chirpy resonation rings the sky,
Of all through which nature proves
Active presence of beauty and brains inside.
