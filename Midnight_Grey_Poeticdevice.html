POETIC DEVICES

Onomatopoeia – “drip‑drip‑drop” mimics sound of rain.

Imagery – auditory atmosphere of rainfall.

Symbolism – rain as persistence and continuity.

Repetition – drops falling endlessly.

Metaphor – rain as time.

Mood – calm, reflective.

Tone – patient, enduring.

Alliteration – "drizzling drip-drip-drop", "Tears trodding", 
"Smoky as clouds sway", "Dim street lamps in the damp", 
"spotlight light that skiddles", "march of the Moon".

Theme – beauty in monotony.

Cycle motif – repetition builds larger rhythms.

Rhyme scheme – aabb (last stanza: abcc)
