🍂 AUTUMNAL GLORY

Slivers of gold through the canopy crown
Tinging a yellow on the bronze and brown,
Jingles the nature and merrily chimes
After yearnings of yearly autumnal climes.

As the contortion of the infinite mile
Adorns in sets of ruby and gold,
It cushions upon the clumsy pearls
Tinting greyish at the silhouette bold.

The scarlet gradience spreads to the sapphire
And the muffled grrounds of bronze leafy shire,
The catalyst breeze blows freckles and flakes
Of dusty worn dry old crumply remains,
Where every rhythm brings about its own story
Of rejuvenating mystiques of autumnal glory.
