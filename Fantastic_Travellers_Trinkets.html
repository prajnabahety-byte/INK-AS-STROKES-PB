🎒 FANTASTIC TRAVELLER'S TRINKETS

Jingling bells tick-tick warm chimes;
Echo in the warm climes,
And wandering traveller traverses the landflow
Scanned by the eyes out the window.

As longingness burries deep within heart
For he wants to run on the dewy grass,
Sad reality just strikes through-
He would really fancy a wheelchair to move.

There hangs his old grey suit,
There lie the coats, socks, pants, new boots,
And souvenirs of his lifetime's stroll
Which he cleans lustrous each nightfall.

His rare collection of assortments include
Corked bottles of items multitude;
The land of deserts brought him sand,
Miniatures and models are from sculptors' land,
Cherries and berries from tribes and clans,
A mercury hourglass and some pending plans.

But still a traveller he swims through
The vast coast of maps and books,
For still his wandering mind admires
The fantastic traveller's trinkets he desired.
