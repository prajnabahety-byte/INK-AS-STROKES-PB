⏰ TICKING TIMES

Tick-tick-tock the antic wall clock,
To-and-fro of the pendulum bob,
Click-click-click an oscillatory assonance,
So-and-so a monotonic resonance.

In the silence casually slips through,
The daybreak hours into nightfall,
Go, you lazy, complete your tasks
For time and tide wait for none at all.
