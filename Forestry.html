🌲 FORESTRY

Woolly-velvety emerald floor,
Craning canopy, Sunlit roof,
Sherry smells and chirping bells;
Rippling crystal water wells.

Pitter and Patter from ballet of clouds,
Tip and Tap of fauna and flowers,
Glinting glory glows in a frame
As jingles the jungle, joyous in the game.
