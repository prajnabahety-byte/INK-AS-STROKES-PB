POETIC DEVICES

Onomatopoeia – “tick‑tick‑tock” mimics clock sound.

Imagery – pendulum, oscillation.

Symbolism – clock as inevitability of time.

Metaphor – time as overseer.

Tone – urgent, commanding.

Mood – reflective yet pressing.

Repetition – “tick‑tick‑tock”, "Click-click-clock", "So-and-so."

Alliteration – “tick‑tick‑tock”, "silence casually slips", "time and tide", "at all."

Theme – inevitability of time’s passage.

Cycle motif – daybreak slipping into nightfall.

Rhyme scheme – aabbcdef
