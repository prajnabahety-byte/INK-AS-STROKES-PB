🕯️ EXPLANATION

This poem is a meditation on nostalgia, solitude, and the way memory reshapes perception. The candle burning by the window becomes a symbol of quiet reflection, its light contrasting with the darkness outside where clouds obscure the stars and moon. The melancholy atmosphere draws the speaker back into recollections of companionship, laughter, and shared adventures, especially the vivid image of racing through mulberry fields. These memories are not presented as distant records but as living emotions that resurface when solitude deepens. The poem emphasizes how circumstances — the dim candlelight, unfinished papers, and the quiet night — alter perception, making the past feel more immediate and poignant. The remembered friend embodies joy and intimacy, her playful nonsense becoming a cherished echo in the speaker’s mind. The contrast between the brightness of memory and the greyness of present loneliness highlights the fragility of human connection. Yet, the act of writing and the glow of candles transform sorrow into creativity, turning melancholy into lyrical expression. The deeper meaning lies in the power of memory to illuminate solitude, showing how remembrance can both wound and heal. Ultimately, the poem celebrates the grandeur of reflection, where the past and present merge into a luminous night of thought.
