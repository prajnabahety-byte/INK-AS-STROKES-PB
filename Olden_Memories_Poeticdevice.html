POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – trunk, dusty photo, veranda.

Symbolism – photo as memory.

Metaphor – trunk as vessel of history.

Tone – nostalgic, tender.

Mood – reflective, bittersweet.

Contrast – forgotten junk vs vivid memory.

Theme – resilience of memory.

Alliteration – "raced in the rain", "scorching sun", "Which I wish."

Repetition – "played and laughed and quarelled always."

Assonance – "of the golden old."

Memory motif – past relived in present.

Rhyme scheme – aabb (2nd stanza: abcc)
