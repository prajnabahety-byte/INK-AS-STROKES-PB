🌧️ EXPLANATION

This poem is a vivid portrayal of rain, but more than that, it is a meditation on the passage of time. The sound of the drops falling one after another creates a rhythm that mirrors the steady ticking of hours. Each drop is small and seemingly insignificant, yet together they form a continuous stream, just as individual moments accumulate into the larger cycles of life. The repetition of the sound emphasizes continuity, reminding us that time does not stop but flows endlessly, sometimes gently, sometimes heavily. The atmosphere of rain immerses the reader, making time feel audible and tangible, as though we can hear life itself moving forward. The poem suggests patience, because rain cannot be rushed; it falls at its own pace, teaching us endurance in the face of monotony. It highlights the quiet passage of moments, showing that beauty can be found in repetition and persistence. The deeper meaning is that life is built from ordinary rhythms, and even the smallest events contribute to the larger story of existence. By focusing on rain, the poet captures the elegance of continuity, turning something simple into a symbol of time’s quiet power. Ultimately, the poem celebrates the harmony between nature and time, inviting us to appreciate the subtle beauty of persistence.
