🏡 HOMEWARD BOUND

Abound beyond the door ajar,
In life to unknown lands afar,
The curious strolls to worthy grands,
Peaks to canyons, endless plans.

All through the drill and thrill,
A smitten mind therefore gets grilled;
Walks back to pavilion for it has found
The pleasure of at last going homeward bound.
