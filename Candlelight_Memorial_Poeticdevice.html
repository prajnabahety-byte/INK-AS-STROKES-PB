🕯️ POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – candle, window, mulberry fields.

Symbolism – candle as reflection, memory.

Metaphor – light as companionship.

Tone – melancholic yet creative.

Mood – intimate, nostalgic.

Contrast – brightness of memory vs greyness of present.

Theme – memory as healing force.

Personification – candle “lights up the room.”

Alliteration – "clouds hold captive", "her holding my hand", "through the","we were". 

Assonance – "of those golden old", "our favoutire spot"

Repetition – "Leisurely by the windowside", "land after land after land".

Cycle motif – past and present merging.

Rhyme scheme – 1.aabb 2.abab 3.abab 4.abab 5.aabbcc
