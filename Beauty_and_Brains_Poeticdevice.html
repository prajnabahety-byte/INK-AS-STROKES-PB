POETIC DEVICES

Imagery – scarlet, amber, ruby, gold.

Symbolism – day/night transitions.

Metaphor – visage as sky.

Tone – majestic, awe‑filled.

Mood – eerie yet celebratory.

Contrast – day’s brightness vs night’s mystery.

Alliteration – “scarlet visage”, "within the woods", 
"stars and squinting", "out once", "resonation rings", 
"beauty and brains"

Theme – grandeur of natural cycles.

Cycle motif – sunrise, sunset, stars, dawn.

Personification – nature “proves beauty and brains.”

Rhyme scheme – free verse
