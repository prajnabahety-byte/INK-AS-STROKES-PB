🌌 EXPLANATION

The poem reflects the eternal rhythm of the cosmos, portraying the sky as a living canvas that shifts through cycles of light and darkness. The ruby and gold symbolize the grandeur of sunsets, while amber suggests fading beauty and the inevitability of decline. The stars and crescent moon embody continuity, offering light in the midst of night. Dawn’s arrival with yellow and blue contrasts reminds us of renewal and rebirth, showing that endings are never final but part of a larger cycle. The semispherical reign of celestial bodies mirrors the journey of life itself, where moments of brilliance and obscurity alternate in harmony. The repetition of imagery emphasizes infinity, suggesting that time is not linear but circular, endlessly looping with charm and wonder. Beneath this cosmic spectacle lies the fragility of earthly existence, hinted at by the “soufflé of mould”, grounding the grandeur in mortality. Yet the cycle’s persistence conveys resilience, assuring that beauty always returns. The poem ultimately celebrates the balance between transience and eternity, reminding us that the universe’s greatest gift is its endless renewal.
