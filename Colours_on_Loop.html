🎨 COLOURS ON LOOP

The contortion of an infinite mile
Adorned in sets of ruby and gold,
A drowning visage of distant amber
Resting upon a soufflé of mould.

A silhouette embedded with twinkle-
Twinkling stars as dots they seem
At the periphery of a solitary crescent-
The moon producing the brightest gleam.

As arises the visage once again 
Contrasting the sky with yellow and blue,
And travels the semispherical reign
Presenting the sky with mystical hues.

As a loop repeating file by file
Endlessly showing the charm it beholds,
The contortion of an infinite mile
Adorned in sets of ruby and gold.
