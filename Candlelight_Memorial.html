🕯️ CANDLELIGHT MEMORIAL

Leisurely by the windowside
With undone papers by my side,
A dying candle lights up the room
And the clouds hold captive the stars and moon.

As the melancholy overwhelmes me a lot
And compels memories of those golden old days,
I remember our favourite spot
Shone by the nightlight and morning rays.

I remember her holding my hand
As we raced through the mulberry fields,
And crossed land after land after land,
Exploring the richness of natural yields.

I remember how she would utter absolute nonsense
And I could listen to her all day,
I miss how we were the best of friends
Which feels reduced to black and grey.

Leisurely by the windowside
With finished lyrics by my side,
And candles that light up the room so bright
Shine as the perfect nightlight,
And thus I relinquish all my woes,
Get carried away by my world of thoughts.
